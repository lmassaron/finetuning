Pulmonary heart disease (Signs and symptoms) : The symptoms/signs of pulmonary heart disease (cor pulmonale) can be non-specific and depend on the stage of the disorder, and can include blood backing up into the systemic venous system, including the hepatic vein. As pulmonary heart disease progresses, most individuals will develop symptoms like: Shortness of breath Wheezing Cyanosis Ascites Jaundice Enlargement of the liver Raised jugular venous pressure (JVP) Third heart sound Intercostal recession Presence of abnormal heart sounds
