Atrial septal defect (Causes) : Down syndrome – patients with Down syndrome have higher rates of ASDs, especially a particular type that involves the ventricular wall. As many as one half of Down syndrome patients have some type of septal defect. Ebstein's anomaly – about 50% of individuals with Ebstein anomaly have an associated shunt between the right and left atria, either an atrial septal defect or a patent foramen ovale. Fetal alcohol syndrome – about one in four patients with fetal alcohol syndrome has either an ASD or a ventricular septal defect. Holt–Oram syndrome – both the ostium secundum and ostium primum types of ASD are associated with Holt–Oram syndrome Lutembacher's syndrome – the presence of a congenital ASD along with acquired mitral stenosis
