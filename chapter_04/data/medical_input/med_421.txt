Pulmonary heart disease (Causes) : The causes of pulmonary heart disease (cor pulmonale) are the following: Acute respiratory distress syndrome (ARDS) COPD Primary pulmonary hypertension Blood clots in lungs/Pulmonary embolism Kyphoscoliosis Interstitial lung disease Cystic fibrosis Sarcoidosis Obstructive sleep apnea (untreated) Sickle cell anemia Bronchopulmonary dysplasia (in infants)
