Beta blocker (Interactions) : Beta blockers have a variety of drug interactions. An example is that various beta blockers including propranolol, carvedilol, nebivolol, timolol, and metoprolol are metabolized by the cytochrome P450 enzyme CYP2D6 and may be potentiated by CYP2D6 inhibitors like fluoxetine, paroxetine, duloxetine, and bupropion. This may increase the risk of adverse effects like bradycardia and hypotension.
