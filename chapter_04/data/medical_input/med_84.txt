Heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (Signs and symptoms) : Clinical manifestations of HFpEF are similar to those observed in HFrEF and include shortness of breath including exercise induced dyspnea, paroxysmal nocturnal dyspnea and orthopnea, exercise intolerance, fatigue, elevated jugular venous pressure, and edema. Patients with HFpEF poorly tolerate stress, particularly hemodynamic alterations of ventricular loading or increased diastolic pressures. In many patients with HFpEF, symptoms are predominantly characterized by exertional dyspnea related to increases in filling pressures during stress, rather than by primary fluid accumulation. Often there is a more dramatic elevation in systolic blood pressure in HFpEF than is typical of HFrEF.
