Pulmonary heart disease (Diagnosis) : Investigations available to determine the cause of cor pulmonale include the following: Chest x-ray – right ventricular hypertrophy, right atrial dilatation, prominent pulmonary artery ECG – right ventricular hypertrophy, dysrhythmia, P pulmonale (characteristic peaked P wave) Thrombophilia screen- to detect chronic venous thromboembolism (proteins C and S, antithrombin III, homocysteine levels)
