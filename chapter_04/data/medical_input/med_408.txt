Patent ductus arteriosus (Prevention) : Some evidence suggests that intravenous NSAIDs, such as indomethacin, administrated on the first day of life to all preterm infants reduces the risk of developing a PDA and the complications associated with PDA. Intravenous indomethacin treatment in premature infants also may reduce the need for surgical intervention. Administering ibuprofen probably helps to prevent PDA and reduce the need for surgery but it also likely increases the risk of kidney complications.
