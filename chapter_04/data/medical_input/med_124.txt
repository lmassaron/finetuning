Cardiac resynchronization therapy (Complications) : Key complications include: Dissection or perforation of coronary sinus which can in turn cause pericardial effusion Inability to cannulate coronary sinus (approximately 5% of patients) Bleeding and pocket haematoma, each of which with an incidence of less than 1% Myocardial perforation, pneumothorax and infection, all of which have an incidence of less than 1%
