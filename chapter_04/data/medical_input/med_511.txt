Anticoagulant (Research) : A substantial number of compounds are being investigated for use as anticoagulants. The most promising ones act on the contact activation system (factor XIIa and factor XIa); it is anticipated that this may provide agents that prevent thrombosis without conferring a risk of bleeding. As of November 2021, the direct factor XIa inhibitor milvexian is in Phase II clinical trials for the prevention of an embolism after surgery.
