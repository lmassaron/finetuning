Takotsubo cardiomyopathy (In popular culture) : In episode 11, Season 3 ("Words and Deeds", 2007) of the TV series House, firefighter Derek suffers from this syndrome. Appeared in episode 1 of season 1 of Astrid et Raphaëlle. The name and title track of the 2021 album Tako Tsubo by French band L'Impératrice refers to the intense emotional stress that may provoke this syndrome.
