Familial hypercholesterolemia (Genetics) : The most common genetic defects in FH are LDLR loss of function mutations (prevalence 1 in 250, depending on the population), ApoB loss of function mutations (prevalence 1 in 1000), PCSK9 gain of function mutations (less than 1 in 2500) and LDLRAP1. The related disease sitosterolemia, which has many similarities with FH and also features cholesterol accumulation in tissues, is due to ABCG5 and ABCG8 mutations.
