Aortic stenosis (Prognosis) : If untreated, severe symptomatic aortic stenosis carries a poor prognosis, with a 2-year mortality rate of 50-60% and a 3-year survival rate of less than 30%. Prognosis after aortic valve replacement for people younger than 65 is about five years less than that of the general population; for people older than 65, it is about the same.
