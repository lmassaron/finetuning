Pulmonary embolism (Prevention) : Pulmonary embolism may be preventable in those with risk factors. People admitted to the hospital may receive preventative medication, including unfractionated heparin, low molecular weight heparin (LMWH), or fondaparinux, and anti-thrombosis stockings to reduce the risk of a DVT in the leg that could dislodge and migrate to the lungs. Following the completion of anticoagulation in those with prior PE, long-term aspirin is useful to prevent recurrence.
