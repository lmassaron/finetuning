Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy (Epidemiology) : The prevalence of hypertrophic cardiomyopathy in the general population globally is 0.2% (1 in 500 adults), as determined by echocardiographic studies. Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy is more common in males than females. The most common presentation of hypertrophic cardiomyopathy is in the third decade of life, though it can present at any age, from newborns to the elderly.
